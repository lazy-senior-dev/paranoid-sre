# Pilot run, 2026-09-04 (kept for the record)

This is the first full run on Claude Code, Codex, and Antigravity, before one calibration pass of the ruleset. It is kept so the effect of that pass can be checked; the numbers in the top-level results file supersede it. The pilot report generated from these replies is next to this file.

What the pilot showed, and what changed because of it:

- The Paranoid SRE caught 15 of 15 seeded defects on Claude Code (the agent alone caught 14), but gave `PAGE` to every one of them, including the seven that hurt on a bad day rather than on rollout (a `latest` tag, missing limits, a probe on a dependency, an all-at-once rollout, a deploy with no concurrency guard, a job with no deadline, an autoscaler removed). `PAGE` precision was 53%. The ruleset now names the `PAGE` classes (data loss, exposure, privilege, a destructive operation with its safety off) and the `HOLD` classes, and says not to promote a `HOLD` because you can imagine the bad day.
- She flagged 4 of 5 clean diffs on Claude Code, against 2 for the agent alone. Reading the replies, the objections were to things outside the diff: a Deployment in another file, an alert not shown, a bucket policy not present, a replica count she could not see. A finding must now cite a line in the change; what the diff does not contain is a one-line question, not a finding, and it does not move the verdict.
- Her replies ran to a median of 3549 output tokens on Claude Code against 738 for the agent alone, and 41 s against 10 s. The ruleset now caps findings at five one-line items with a one-clause fix.

Raw replies for the pilot are in `raw/` next to this file: Claude Code and Codex are complete (n=2), Antigravity is complete (n=1). The persona arm was rerun on the revised rules; the other two arms did not change and were kept.

A second, smaller pass followed the first rerun: the persona arm still held two clean diffs, once for a fixed disruption budget under an autoscaler and a versioned bucket with no expiry for old versions (fair by her rules, so those two clean diffs were tightened), and once for alerts that would live in another system (the ruleset now says such an alert is named in one line, never held for). The persona arm was rerun after each change; the bare and generic arms were kept.
